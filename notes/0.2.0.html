Sermonship 0.2.0

  • Ask for what you need. Each sermon's page now has Videos, Images and Documents workspaces, each with a Create Custom button: describe the clip, the quote card or the document you want in a sentence and Sermonship reads the whole sermon and makes it. Clips take a length (short, standard or long) and can be cut from a passage you choose; documents follow the brief, however small; cards come at any shape on any background.
  • About twice as fast with the built-in writing model. The sermon is transcribed on the Mac's GPU, the clips are cut while the documents are being written, and the model thinks for as long as each step needs rather than longer. A one-hour sermon now takes about ten minutes on a recent Mac.
  • Smaller things: a short highlight list is asked for again instead of being accepted; the shape picker shows a preview; custom cards no longer count as part of the quote-card set; the workspaces fit the window at its smallest size.
