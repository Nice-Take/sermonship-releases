Sermonship 0.1.0

The first release, for a small group of testers.

  • Everything from one recording: transcript, two summaries, ten discussion questions, five daily devotionals, quotes and quote cards, and vertical highlight clips with and without subtitles.
  • Writes on your Mac with a model it downloads once; or with your own Gemini key.
  • Choose the whole suite or just some of it, and add more later, including more clips.
  • Ask the Sermon: questions, drafts and a clip about any subject, from the transcript.
  • Customise: card shape, backgrounds, theme colour, logo (with strength), speaker name, your own notes to the writer.
