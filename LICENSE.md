## 5. Privacy and your own API key

Sermonship works on your Mac. In its standard setting the video, audio, transcript and everything written from them stay on your Mac; the writing model runs locally and nothing about your sermons is sent anywhere. The one thing Sermonship fetches from the internet is the writing model itself, downloaded once from Hugging Face when you choose it (Hugging Face sees that request as it would any download). Sermonship has no account, collects no information about you and sends nothing to NiceTake.

If you choose to use your own Google Gemini API key, Sermonship sends the text of the transcript (never the video or audio) to Google to be written from. That use is governed by your agreement with Google, not by this licence, and any charges are between you and Google.
